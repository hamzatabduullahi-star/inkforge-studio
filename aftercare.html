Heal it right.

Six things to do (and not do) for the first 3–4 weeks.
Follow these and your tattoo will settle beautifully.

01

Keep it wrapped

Leave the film on for 2–4 hours. Peel it off gently — never rip.

02

Moisturize daily

Thin layer of unscented balm 2–3× a day for two weeks. Less is more.

03

Gentle washing

Lukewarm water, fragrance-free soap, twice a day. Pat dry — don't rub.

04

Avoid sun & water

No pools, oceans or saunas for 3 weeks. SPF 50 on it for the first year.

05

Don't pick or scratch

Itching is normal. Tap, don't scratch. Picking pulls pigment.

06

Touch-up policy

One free touch-up within 90 days of your session. Just message me.

IF ANYTHING FEELS OFF

Hit me up. I'd rather hear from you early than worry quietly.
